## Preview and final QA

Record rendered revision, composition, entry, dimensions, FPS, frame range,
renderer, concurrency, render time, output SHA-256 and downloadable artifact URL.
Inspect wide views, close faces, action peaks, transitions, shadows, blur and ink.
Check full decoding, no missing/frozen/black frames, frame count, duration, audio
start/end and cue timing, speech intelligibility and all factual claims. Record
known caveats; ask for preview approval before proposing the final export.
